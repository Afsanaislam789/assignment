Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DevConf 2026.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes, all keynote talks and technical sessions will be recorded. Standard pass holders get 30 days of access, while Pro and Team pass holders get lifetime access.

Can I upgrade my ticket later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from a Standard to a Pro pass at any time before October 15, 2026, by paying the price difference, provided tickets are still available.

Are there any team or student discounts?

Yes! We offer a specialized Team package for up to 10 people at a discounted rate. For student discounts, please contact us with a valid institutional ID.

Where is the venue located?

DevConf 2026 will take place at the Grand Tech Pavilion Center, downtown. Detailed transit and parking information will be emailed to all registered attendees.
